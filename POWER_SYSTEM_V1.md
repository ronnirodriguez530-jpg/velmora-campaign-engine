# Velmora Power System V1

## Working scope

The first power checkpoint provides engine-readable power definitions, persistent player ownership, validated acquisition sources, sustained-power activation, event history, player-context retrieval, save compatibility, and one-turn rollback.

The initial catalog is deliberately small. It converts seven already approved concepts from the larger content catalog into bounded records without treating every idea-bank entry as finished mechanics.

## Protected rules

- A player may maintain only one sustained magical effect at a time. Activating another ends the previous effect.
- Instant and fixed-duration effects do not occupy the sustained-effect slot.
- Unconsciousness will end a sustained effect once conditions and unconsciousness exist.
- Taking damage while sustaining magic will require a DC 12 Constitution save. Critical damage will impose disadvantage. This is recorded but cannot be implemented until damage and conditions exist.
- Level 3 powers must originate from the central Tear or a void-rift.
- Permanent Level 3 acquisition requires an approved opportunity, explicit character choice, engine validation, and player approval.
- Movement powers cannot create unrestricted portals, leave Velmora, or turn the central Tear into an outward travel route.
- A power cannot supply numeric combat authority before combat, damage, and condition rules exist.

## Initial executable definitions

- Short Blink
- Rift Sight
- Silent Movement
- Heat Siphon
- Force Shield
- Phase Walk
- World Anchor

These definitions establish ownership and narrative boundaries. They do not yet provide damage, healing, defense bonuses, action costs, charges, or progression upgrades.

- Upgrade paths and scaling limits
- Broader catalog conversion after the minimum system is proven
