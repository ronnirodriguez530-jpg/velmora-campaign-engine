# Velmora Power and Creature Content Catalog

## Status

This file is approved content direction, not implemented mechanics. It consolidates the submitted idea banks into a usable Velmora foundation without silently giving the Campaign Master unrestricted authority.

Exact statistics, costs, ownership, encounter rules, and advancement remain deferred to their proper systems.

## Selection rules

- Keep abilities readable in theater-of-the-mind play.
- Combine close duplicates as techniques inside one power family.
- Use Level 1 for common/minor effects, Level 2 for uncommon/significant effects, and Level 3 for rare/major effects.
- Reserve time, probability, alternate-self, resurrection, unrestricted portal, and permanent mind-control effects for later plot approval.
- Creatures originate only as altered native life or void-rift arrivals.
- A creature name without a behavior hook remains a reserve name, not playable content.

### 11. Nature and creature interaction

Usable concepts: Animal Command, Insect Command, Plant Growth, Fungus Growth, Creature Taming, Monster Scent, Beast Form, Predator Adaptation, and Temporary Trait Copying.

These powers interact with existing native or void-arrived creatures; they do not manufacture new creature species.

Power Copy, Power Borrowing, Power Echo, Contact Mimicry, Trait Mirror, Ability Archive, and Skill Borrowing require strict duration, capacity, and strength limits before player use.

- Make an Object Return to a Previous State
- Make Distance Shorter Between Two Points
- Send an Object Outside Reality and Recall It
- Store a Spell and Release It Later
- Turn Shadows Solid

## Restricted later concepts

These ideas are preserved but excluded from the first playable power catalog because they can break continuity, player agency, encounter resolution, or the compact-city boundary:

- Personal Rewind, Object Rewind, Event Replay, Causal Delay, Delayed Damage, and repeating-moment traps
- Luck Shift, Misfortune Curse, Discarded Outcome, and direct probability rewriting
- Alternate-Self Memory, Alternate-Self Summon, Parallel Draft, Dead-Self Recall, Reality Shed, Echo Life, and dead alternate bodies
- Unrestricted Portal Opening, World Blend, Worldskin, Foreign Law, Reality Scar, Rift Infection, Room Fold, Spatial Snare, and forced location overlap
- Corpse Animation, unrestricted Spirit Summon, Full Possession, and resurrection-like effects
- Permanent Memory Theft, Memory Implantation, Thought Seed, and compulsory Voice Command
- Unlimited Power Copy, Ability Archive, or Power Borrowing
- Pulling creatures or arbitrary objects from alternate worlds on demand

They may later become unique relic, entity, boss, consequence, or one-use story effects after separate approval.

## Later set-piece creatures

These are usable, but should enter only after the city, combat, and scale rules can support them:

- **Cathedral Walker** — gigantic slow creature carrying an ecosystem on its back.
- **Room Whale** — enormous being capable of occupying an interior smaller than its body.
- **Quiet Giant** — massive destructive creature that produces no sound.
- **Echo Giant** — each movement produces smaller delayed copies.
- **Rift Shepherd** — attracts and directs groups of smaller dimensional creatures.
- **Skinless Saint** — regenerating humanoid entity with unfamiliar organs.
- **Parallel Predator** — hunts one target across several realities.
- **Inside Beast** — most of its body remains elsewhere while selected limbs enter reality.
- **Hallway Eater** — inhabits corridors and makes them progressively longer.
- **Room-scale Fold Beast** — distorts space around its body and requires a dedicated encounter.
