# Velmora Relic and Magic-Tech Content Catalog

## Status

This file contains curated content direction, not implemented item mechanics. Exact statistics, charges, prices, ownership, crafting, equipment rules, and Campaign Master authority remain deferred to the inventory, economy, and ability systems.

## Selection rules

- Prefer items that support investigation, survival, containment, faction conflict, creature hunting, and rebuilding Velmora.
- Keep effects short enough for theater-of-the-mind play.
- Require approved limits, charges, costs, or risks before mechanical use.
- Reserve unrestricted portals, alternate outcomes, large reality changes, time manipulation, and soul exploitation for later plot approval.
- Magic-tech may be refined institutional equipment or rough post-apocalyptic scrap construction.

## Foundation relics

### Containment and defense

- **Null Lantern** — suppresses magic inside its light.
- **Moon Nail** — pins ghosts or phased creatures in place.
- **Warden Chain** — weakens a supernatural creature it binds.
- **Iron Scripture** — protects against one named supernatural effect at a time.
- **Salt Crown** — protects against possession and spirit intrusion.
- **Pale Banner** — makes undead and summoned creatures hesitate before crossing beneath it.
- **Hearth Stone** — creates a small safe zone that hostile creatures avoid unless provoked.
- **Cinder Rosary** — each bead absorbs one burst of heat or flame.

### Investigation and tracking

- **Blood Compass** — points toward a chosen living person after tasting their blood.
- **Bone Dial** — points toward nearby undead or spirits.
- **Thief's Eye** — reveals hidden compartments, traps, and false walls.
- **Rift Needle** — points toward weak places between dimensions.
- **Memory Stone** — stores one memory that can be replayed through touch.
- **Silent Bell** — cannot be heard normally, but spirits react to it.
- **Black Mirror** — reveals supernatural entities that are otherwise invisible.
- **Saint's Coin** — warms when someone nearby lies with harmful intent.
- **Dead Man's Watch** — stops when someone nearby dies.
- **Whisper Jar** — captures spoken words and releases them later.

### Healing, repair, and survival

- **Black Thread** — stitches wounds, objects, or damaged enchantments together.
- **Glass Heart** — keeps a mortally wounded person alive until the relic shatters.
- **Saint's Rib** — slowly heals its carrier.
- **Mercy Blade** — prevents its wounds from becoming infected or cursed.
- **Scarlet Thread** — links two wounds so healing one partly heals the other.
- **Pilgrim Boots** — reduce fatigue while traveling toward a declared destination inside the playable world.

### Conflict, vows, and faction pressure

- **Ash Crown** — commands fire that already exists.
- **Echo Knife** — repeats its most recent wound after a brief delay.
- **Storm Bell** — calls localized lightning when rung outdoors.
- **Red Ledger** — records every oath spoken nearby.
- **King's Nail** — makes a pierced target or object extremely difficult to move.
- **War Drum** — strengthens nearby courage and physical endurance.
- **Gallows Rope** — tightens as its restrained target struggles.
- **Oathbreaker Blade** — grows stronger against someone who broke a sworn promise.

### Controlled utility and anomaly handling

- **Hollow Chalice** — drains magical energy from what is poured into it.
- **Sun Shard** — stores daylight and releases it as heat or blinding light.
- **Hunter's Veil** — hides scent, heat, and magical presence.
- **Frost Nail** — freezes the material into which it is hammered.
- **Void Cup** — temporarily removes poured material before returning it.
- **Maw Box** — consumes small objects and later returns altered versions; use requires a bounded result table.
- **Grave Key** — unlocks approved tombs, wards, or spirit-bound doors rather than every seal automatically.

## High-tier rift relics

These are Level 3, tightly controlled, unique, or plot-linked relic directions:

- **World Anchor** — blocks portals, teleportation, and phasing nearby.
- **Rift Spine** — grants temporary resistance to foreign physics.
- **Impossible Compass** — points toward something the bearer genuinely needs rather than merely wants.
- **Void Lantern** — reveals dimensional creatures and hidden breaches.
- **Second Skin** — replaces damaged flesh with material from another reality.
- **Horizon Ring** — shortens travel toward something visible without leaving Velmora.
- **Static Bible** — displays changing survival instructions for nearby anomalies.
- **Rift Hook** — drags partially phased entities fully into reality.
- **World Needle** — sews a small dimensional breach shut.
- **Empty Mask** — makes the wearer seem unimportant to many supernatural senses.
- **Unmade Stone** — temporarily removes one magical property from an object.
- **Fracture Orb** — divides one magical effect into weaker copies.
- **Black Atlas** — maps locations from other realities that overlap Velmora, without enabling outward exploration.
- **Void Seed** — produces a temporary bounded pocket space rather than a travel route.
- **Folded Sword** — strikes around corners or through thin space.

## Restricted later relics

These concepts are preserved for unique quests, entities, bosses, or consequences, but are excluded from the base item pool:

- **Elsewhere Key** and **Doorless Frame** — unrestricted dimensional routes.
- **Parallel Coin** and **Split Clock** — alternate-outcome manipulation.
- **Thirteen-Sided Die** and **Seven-Door Bell** — uncontrolled anomaly generation.
- **Foreign Crown** and **Dead Sun Lens** — large-scale foreign-physics effects.
- **Black Egg** — unknown creature release without an approved creature entry.
- **Paradox Bullet** and **Elseblade** — continuity-breaking attacks.
- **Reverse Mirror** — undefined alternate-potential rewriting.
- **Rift Coffin** — time preservation and death-system implications.
- **Ashen Hourglass** — time manipulation.
- **Traveler's Tooth** — return travel that could bypass the approved movement graph.
- **Fool's Die** — uncontrolled effect redirection.
- **Dream Mask** — usable later after dream and consent rules exist.
- **Rot Idol** and **Ruin Seed** — structural or biological destruction requiring consequence rules.

## Foundation institutional magic-tech

### Detection and analysis

- **Spirit Scanner** — detects ghosts, possession, and soul residue.
- **Rift Detector** — measures dimensional instability.
- **Magic Dosimeter** — tracks supernatural exposure.
- **Ghost Radio** — converts spirit activity into audible signals.
- **Spell Recorder** — captures a spell's structure for analysis.
- **Blood Reader** — identifies magical traits in a blood sample.
- **Mutation Scanner** — maps supernatural biological changes.
- **Relic Interface** — lets electronics communicate with compatible relics.

### Energy and fabrication

- **Mana Battery** — stores raw magical energy.
- **Spell Capacitor** — charges a spell gradually for a stronger bounded release.
- **Rune Printer** — burns standardized magical symbols onto surfaces.
- **Enchanted Ammunition Press** — produces approved specialized rounds.
- **Artifact Charger** — replenishes relics designed to store magic.
- **Magic Relay** — transfers magical energy between connected devices.
- **Magic Welder** — bonds enchanted materials while preserving their properties.
- **Alchemy Synthesizer** — produces approved basic potions from chemical and magical feedstock.
- **Mana Siphon** — collects ambient magic into a battery within safe limits.

### Wards and containment

- **Ward Generator** — projects a barrier around a room or vehicle.
- **Sigil Projector** — places temporary wards onto nearby surfaces.
- **Ward Beacon** — marks territory that specified creatures avoid.
- **Binding Collar** — suppresses specified supernatural abilities.
- **Power Dampener** — weakens powers within a small radius.
- **Hex Scrubber** — removes weak curses from objects.
- **Curse Extractor** — transfers weak curses into disposable cartridges.
- **Possession Lock** — blocks spirits from entering a protected body or machine.
- **Rune Lock** — combines electronic and magical access control.
- **Magic Jammer** — interferes with spellcasting in a limited area.
- **Ward Door** — rejects specified creatures or powers.
- **Anomaly Cage** — contains small approved supernatural objects or creatures.
- **Rift Stabilizer** — temporarily prevents a void-rift from expanding.
- **Void Shielding** — reduces dimensional contamination.

### Field and combat equipment

- **Rune Rifle** — uses interchangeable approved spell chambers.
- **Hex Mine** — triggers a stored curse under defined conditions.
- **Rune Armor** — powered armor reinforced with magical inscriptions.
- **Kinetic Ward Vest** — converts part of an impact into stored magical charge.
- **Elemental Thrower** — uses approved fire, cold, lightning, or acid cores.
- **Charm Grenade** — produces a brief bounded emotional or illusion effect.
- **Rift Harpoon** — anchors a dimensional creature in normal space.
- **Phase Cutter** — harms partially intangible targets.
- **Rune Drone** — carries wards, traps, scanners, or spell modules.
- **Familiar Harness** — lets a trained creature carry approved magic-tech modules.
- **Spell Turret** — fires one stored approved magical attack.
- **Spell Repeater** — reproduces a weaker version of a recorded approved spell.
- **Hex Firewall** — protects machinery from supernatural interference.

- Shotgun fitted with hand-carved recoil wards
- Battery pack wired into a lightning crystal
- Gas mask with spirit-sight lenses
- Scrap-metal prosthetic animated by runes
- Backpack ward generator assembled from scavenged appliances
- Nailgun firing iron ward spikes
- Homemade radio that detects rift static
- Chainsaw with an enchanted chain for supernatural flesh
- Flashlight powered by bottled ghost-light
- Car battery converted into a mana cell
- Welding mask that protects against magical gaze effects
- Warded cart for transporting dangerous artifacts
- Homemade turret using spell-stamped ammunition
- Modified CRT monitor that displays nearby spirits
- Tattoo gun that installs temporary approved combat runes
- Refrigerator converted into a cold relic-containment locker
- Speaker system broadcasting anti-spirit frequencies
- Crossbow firing tether bolts that interrupt phasing
- Scrap drone carrying curse-detection charms
- Riot shield fitted with a rechargeable barrier rune
- Portable cage constructed from mismatched relic metals
- Engine block engraved to repel specified rift creatures

## Restricted or illegal magic-tech

- **Soul Battery** — ethically dangerous spiritual-energy storage.
- **Spirit Trap** — captures ghosts or soul fragments.
- **Magic Injector** and liquid-magic IV rigs — direct exposure and alteration risk.
- **Portable Portal Rig** — conflicts with the controlled void-rift and movement boundaries.
- **Rift Agitator** — deliberately destabilizes a dimensional weak point.
- **Dimensional Beacon** — may attract unknown foreign attention.
- Generators that burn enchanted material or trapped spirits as fuel.

These may exist as contraband, faction secrets, hazards, or quest objects, but do not enter ordinary equipment lists without later approval.

## Content boundary

This catalog supplies approved content and reserves. It does not yet create item records, equipment slots, crafting, prices, charges, ownership, loot tables, or usable mechanics. Those remain absent until their dedicated systems are implemented and tested.
