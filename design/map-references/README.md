# Velmora Map Reference Set

The four supplied images in this folder are visual references for a consistent Velmora map family. They establish the circular silhouette, six wedge districts, Council Crown at north, central containment structure, radial streets, outer defenses, and the repeated clockwise faction order.

## Canonical map order

Clockwise from the Council Crown:

1. Aurorus Circle — 12 to 2 o'clock
2. House Chrysalis — 2 to 4 o'clock
3. Hands of Velmora — 4 to 6 o'clock
4. League of Thorns — 6 to 8 o'clock
5. Wayfarer Pact — 8 to 10 o'clock
6. Order of Glass — 10 to 12 o'clock

## Reference boundary

The overall structure, faction order, radial circulation, outer wall, gates, guard posts, and central defensive ring may guide the final map. Labels such as "Forbidden Zone" and "Quarantine Ring," exact building placement, road counts, measurements, and small defense notes are reference details only unless separately approved in campaign data.
